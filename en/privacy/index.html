At a glance

This website deliberately avoids:

  • Cookies for analytics or marketing purposes
  • External fonts (e.g. Google Fonts) — only fonts already installed on your device are used
  • Embedded third-party widgets or maps

We only process data that is technically necessary to run the website, plus any data you actively share with us via the contact form or other channels (phone, email, WhatsApp).

Hosting & server log files

This website is hosted by an external hosting provider [TODO: add the host's name, address and a link to their privacy policy, e.g. once hosting with IONOS or similar is finalised]. When you visit the site, the hosting provider automatically collects so-called server log files transmitted by your browser, such as IP address, date and time of the request, browser type/version and operating system. This data is technically unavoidable and is not combined with other data sources. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, functional website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Contact form

If you use the contact form, we process the data you provide (name, email address, message) solely to handle your inquiry and any follow-up questions. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual inquiry) and/or Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (your consent via the checkbox). The data is deleted once it is no longer required to process your inquiry, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

Contact via phone, email or WhatsApp

If you contact us by phone or email, we process the resulting data (e.g. phone number, email address, message content) to handle your request, based on our legitimate interest in efficient communication (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

For contact via WhatsApp, we use the WhatsApp service operated by WhatsApp Ireland Limited (a Meta company). If you contact us via the WhatsApp link, your message and technical metadata (e.g. phone number, device data) are transmitted to and processed by WhatsApp/Meta under their own privacy terms, including outside the EU in some cases. Using WhatsApp is entirely voluntary and only happens if you actively click the corresponding link.

External links (shop, Reico, Instagram)

This website links to external offerings (Mad Dogs Shop, the Reico Vital-Systeme partner page, our Instagram profile). Clicking these links takes you away from our website; these are plain hyperlinks, not embedded content. Data processing on these external sites is governed exclusively by the respective provider's own privacy policy.

Cookies & tracking

This website does not use cookies for analytics, marketing or tracking purposes, and does not embed any corresponding third-party services. No user profiles are created.

Your rights as a data subject

Subject to applicable law, you have the right at any time to:

  • request information about your stored personal data (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • request correction of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • request deletion of your stored data (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • request restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • withdraw any consent given, with effect for the future

To exercise these rights, simply contact us at the email address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, e.g. the data protection authority responsible for North Rhine-Westphalia.

Changes to this privacy policy

We will update this privacy policy whenever the website or the services we use change. The version published on this page is always the current, applicable one.
