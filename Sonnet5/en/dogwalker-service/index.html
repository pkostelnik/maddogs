Dog Walking Service in Niederkassel

Professional Dog Walking – Even for Demanding Dogs

Do you have a dog with lots of energy, a strong will, or a lot of character? Mad Dogs offers more than a walk: structured enrichment, clear guidance and individual attention.

Get in touch on WhatsApp (opens in a new tab) Call 0173 3649143

Ideal for dogs that…

  • have plenty of energy
  • pull on the leash
  • get worked up around other dogs
  • need clear rules and real outlets

What your dog gets from me

  • Calm, consistent guidance
  • Focused enrichment for body & mind
  • Walks in small, well-matched groups of up to 10 dogs
  • Social skills training and impulse control while out and about
  • Reliability & experience handling "strong personalities"

Background

16 years of experience working with dogs

I've been working with dogs of all kinds of breeds in dog sports for 16 years. I spent 1.5 years working at one of Cologne's largest dog daycare centres, supervising and walking dogs in large groups. I'm also active as a service dog handler and have completed my dog trainer training.

Enrichment that works the mind, not just the legs.
