Is this right for your dog?

When a normal walk around the block isn't enough anymore

The dog walking service is aimed especially at dogs with a lot of temperament – but also at anyone looking for dependable care with a clear structure.

  • Dogs with plenty of energy and a mind of their own
  • Dogs who pull on the leash or get worked up around other dogs
  • Owners who value clear rules and real physical/mental outlets
