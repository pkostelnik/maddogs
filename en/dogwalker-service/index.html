Dog walking service, Niederkassel

Professional dog walking — even for demanding dogs

Got a dog with lots of energy, a strong will, or a big personality? Mad Dogs offers more than just a walk: structured activity, calm leadership and individual attention.

Get in touch on WhatsApp Call 0173 3649143

Great for dogs that …

  • have a lot of energy
  • pull on the leash
  • get overexcited around other dogs
  • need clear rules and real mental/physical outlets

What your dog gets

  • Calm, consistent guidance
  • Purposeful outlets for body & mind
  • Walks in small, well-matched groups of up to 10 dogs
  • Social training and impulse control out on walks
  • Reliability & experience handling "strong personalities"

Background

16 years of experience working with dogs

I've been handling dogs of many different breeds in dog sport for 16 years. I worked for 1.5 years at one of Cologne's largest dog daycare centres, supervising and walking dogs in large groups. I also work as a service dog handler and have completed my training as a certified dog trainer.

Walks with a plan — for dogs that need a bit more.
