Is this the right fit?

When your dog needs more than a lap around the block

The dog walking service is especially suited to high-spirited dogs — but also to anyone looking for reliable care with a clear structure.

  • Dogs with lots of energy and a strong will
  • Dogs that pull on the leash or get worked up around other dogs
  • Owners who value clear rules and genuine mental/physical outlets
