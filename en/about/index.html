About us

Chris Knittel – dog trainer, dog walker & service dog handler

Mad Dogs Germany isn't a corporation — it's one person with a clear approach: Chris Knittel has competed in dog sports since 2014, works as a service dog handler in civilian and military security services, and has additionally trained as a Pet/Man Trail instructor and certified dog trainer.

This mix of competitive sport, professional security-service practice and pedagogical training shapes the way he works: calm, consistent, and attentive to dogs that need a bit more guidance — whether in competition, in training, or on a daily walk.

Background

Milestones in dog sport

  1. since 2014

    Started in competitive dog sport

    Active competitor with SWHV, VDH national championship and DHV national championship starts, plus more than 70 competition starts in THS (German agility-style dog sport).

  2. 2017

    Runner-up, German Championship GL 2000

    Awarded the City of Metzingen's honorary prize for outstanding achievement in dog sport.

  3. 2018

    3rd place SWHV CSC & 3rd place SWHV GL 2000m

    Again awarded the City of Metzingen's honorary prize for outstanding achievement in dog sport.

  4. ongoing

    Protection-work helper within the club

    Supports club-level protection-work training and brings experience working with anxious or reactive dogs.

Training & qualifications

Properly trained, tested in practice

Pet/Man Trail instructor

Nine-month training programme with Pet Trailer in Austria — the professional foundation for the mantrailing training offered at Mad Dogs Germany.

Service dog handler

Active in civilian and military security services — hands-on experience in handling, obedience and nose work under real-world conditions.

Certified dog trainer

Trained as a dog trainer with Kynologisch, complemented by specific knowledge of working with anxious and reactive dogs.

Dog walker with pack experience

1.5 years working at one of Cologne's largest dog daycare centres — supervising and walking dogs in large groups.
